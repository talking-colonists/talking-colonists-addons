# Changelog

## Unreleased

- Speaking up at the fire works: right-click the fire with an empty hand and speak, or type in chat. The tellers stop and listen, the one you look at answers you, then the stories go on. Before, they talked on as if you had said nothing.

## 0.1.0

- First version: at dusk, idle citizens gather around a lit campfire in the colony and tell each other stories from their memories. Players nearby listen, and can join in by chatting. `/campfire start` starts one right away.
- A chapter in Talking Colonists' Colony Handbook explains how to use it, and citizens know it too, so you can ask them. When it becomes relevant, a citizen walks up and tells you about it once (Talking Colonists versions with the handbook and introductions).
