# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- First version: the Ballot Box block, with a window to stand for mayor, give a speech and watch a live tally; or stand by right-clicking your colony's Town Hall block with a signed book (title: slogan, text: platform), then give a speech through Simple Voice Chat. Citizens spread what they heard; a citizen stands against a lone candidate; every grown citizen votes in character on what they heard and how they feel about each candidate. The result is announced to the colony and a citizen brings you the results book. Also the Suggestion Box block, where unhappy citizens drop a short note each morning, read in its own window.
- A chapter in Talking Colonists' Colony Handbook explains how to use it, and citizens know it too, so you can ask them (Talking Colonists versions with the handbook).
