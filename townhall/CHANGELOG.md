# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- First version: stand for mayor by right-clicking your colony's Town Hall block with a signed book (title: slogan, text: platform), then give a speech through Simple Voice Chat. Citizens spread what they heard; a citizen stands against a lone candidate; every grown citizen votes in character on what they heard and how they feel about each candidate. The result is announced to the colony and a citizen brings you the results book. Also a suggestion box: a barrel next to the Town Hall block, where unhappy citizens drop a short note each morning.
