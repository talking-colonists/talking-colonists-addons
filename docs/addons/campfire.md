# Campfire Nights

At dusk, idle citizens gather around a lit campfire in the colony, sit down and take turns telling
stories: memories, colony news, tales from before they came. It is a real Gemini Live group
conversation, so you hear them talk, and you can join in.

## How to start

1. Place a **campfire** inside your colony and keep it lit.
2. Be near it at dusk (in-game time 12000 to 13500). Three to five idle citizens from nearby walk
   over and sit around the fire.
3. Listen as they tell their stories in turn. To join in, right-click the fire with an empty hand
   and speak (Simple Voice Chat), or type in chat while you stand near the fire (within about 24
   blocks). The tellers stop and listen, the one you look at answers you, and then the stories go
   on.

When the night is over, the tellers stand up and go back to their evening.

## Good to know

- At most one campfire night per colony and day, and only when Talking Colonists has spare speech
  capacity (Gemini Live sessions).
- Citizens who sleep, work or talk to someone else stay away. Tellers come from within about 48
  blocks of the fire.
- The night becomes colony news, so the [Colony Gazette](gazette.md) may report it.

## For operators

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/campfire start` | Starts a night at the nearest lit campfire right away. |
| `/campfire stop` | Ends the running night. |

Both need permission level 2.

## Settings

Campfire Nights has no settings of its own. How many voices can speak at once comes from Talking
Colonists' capacity settings.
