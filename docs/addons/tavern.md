# Tavern Recruiter

MineColonies visitors at the tavern can be talked to like citizens. Each one knows what joining the
colony costs, and a good conversation can lower the price.

## How to start

1. Build a **tavern** and wait for a visitor.
2. Talk to the visitor the same way as to any citizen (the Citizen Communication Device, or the
   talk gesture).
3. Make a good case: a job that suits them, kindness, a fair offer. A convinced visitor lowers their
   recruit cost.
4. Recruit them in MineColonies' recruit window at the new price.

## Good to know

- The price drops in small steps, never below half of the original, and changes at most three times
  a day. The server enforces these limits, whatever the conversation.
- Once they join, they remember the deal.

## For operators

There are no commands. Deals are stored in `<world>/data/tc_tavern.json`.

## Settings

The Tavern Recruiter has no settings of its own.
